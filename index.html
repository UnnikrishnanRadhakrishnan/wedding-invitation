Our Story

Our story began within the walls of a university, where a chance encounter quietly set the course for something extraordinary.

What started with shared stories, endless conversations, and effortless laughter soon blossomed into a bond that felt both unexpected and familiar. In each other, we found more than friends - we found a confidant, a companion, and a place to call home.

Together, we embraced life's joys and weathered its challenges, standing side by side through every high and low. Each moment, whether simple or significant, became a thread in the tapestry of our journey, weaving a story of friendship, trust, resilience, and a love that grew stronger with every step.

What began as two paths crossing became one beautiful journey, filled with cherished memories, unwavering support, and countless reasons to be grateful. And now, with hearts full of love and hope, we choose each other, not just for today, but for every tomorrow that follows.

This is our story. And this is only the beginning of our forever.
